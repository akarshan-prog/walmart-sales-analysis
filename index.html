Key insights

  • Sales peak in the weeks before Christmas. The flagged "Christmas" week (31 Dec) is a post-holiday dip, so the holiday flag understates the real peak.
  • Thanksgiving is the strongest flagged holiday, about 40% above a normal week.
  • Type A stores sell the most per department-week ($20.1K), but small Type C stores earn the most per sq ft.
  • Markdowns have a weak link to sales (correlation 0.11). Temperature, fuel price, CPI and unemployment barely move sales.
  • Stock and staff for November and December first. Departments 72, 6 and 18 need the most holiday planning.
Data: Kaggle Walmart Recruiting – Store Sales Forecasting. 421,570 weekly records. Negative sales rows (1,285) are kept as returns.
